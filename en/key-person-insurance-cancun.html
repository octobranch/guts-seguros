A short answer

What is key person insurance?

Key person insurance is designed to support a business against the financial impact of a covered event affecting an essential person. The insured person, policyholder, beneficiary, benefit amount and covered risks must all be defined.

A guide to your decision

How to identify a key person in your business

A key person is not necessarily a director. Look at who holds important customer relationships, technical skills or decision-making knowledge that would be hard to replace.

  • Identify functions with no immediate replacement.
  • Separate the commercial impact from hiring and training costs.
  • Review who holds essential relationships, authorisations or knowledge.
  • Relate the proposed benefit amount to a documented financial assessment.

If you are in Cancun

In a Cancun business, risk may be concentrated in someone who manages clients, leads operations or holds technical knowledge. These are examples to assess, not automatically accepted profiles. The insurer determines its requirements and assessment.

Frequently asked questions: Key person insurance

Discuss my situation
Who can be a key person?

It may be someone whose absence would materially affect the business’s operations or finances. Eligibility is not determined by job title alone; the relationship with the company and product requirements need review.

Does it cover death and disability?

Covered risks depend on the product and purchased benefits. Disability or any work absence should not be assumed to be covered. Request the definition and terms of each covered event.

How is the benefit amount determined?

It should relate to the financial impact and the insurer’s assessment criteria. Evidence of the need may be required; this website does not apply automatic multipliers or set one amount for every company.

Does it cover every business risk?

No. Key person protection addresses a specific risk. It is not property, liability or group insurance, nor a complete continuity plan. Review your other operational risks separately.

Does it automatically provide tax benefits?

Do not assume so. Any tax treatment depends on the circumstances, contract and applicable requirements. A review needs documentation and case-specific tax advice; this page does not promise deductibility.
