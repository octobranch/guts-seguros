A short answer

What is education insurance?

Education insurance combines a savings goal for studies with the protection specified in the policy. It sets out a term, contributions and benefits for designated people. Plans can differ in how they address death, disability, early withdrawal or changes to contributions.

A guide to your decision

How to define a university savings goal

Start with the goal, rather than an advertised return. Write down the expenses you want to support and when the funds would be needed.

  • Separate tuition from enrolment, materials and other expenses.
  • Review how long it is until studies begin and how many years they may last.
  • Consider possible changes in costs, currency and study location.
  • Compare the contribution commitment with what you can sustainably afford.

If you are in Cancun

For a family in Cancun, the goal may include studying locally or elsewhere. Defining this helps avoid comparing proposals based on different expenses. Buying education insurance does not automatically include a place at a university, tuition entitlement or scholarship.

Frequently asked questions: Education insurance

Discuss my situation
Does education insurance guarantee any university?

Do not assume so. The contract sets benefits and conditions; it does not guarantee admission or that the funds will cover any institution. Define your goal and compare agreed benefits with expected expenses.

How much does education insurance in Cancun cost?

It depends on the goal, term, contributions, insured person’s profile and chosen benefits. Request a proposal distinguishing protection costs, savings and payment terms.

What happens if I cannot keep contributing?

This depends on the product’s terms. Before purchasing, ask about contribution changes, payment periods, cancellation and surrender values. Do not assume all contributions can be recovered.

What happens if the contributor dies?

Check who is insured and which benefits were purchased. Some products provide protection or waive contributions after specified events; the policy must set out how this applies.

Are education insurance and savings insurance the same?

They can share savings and protection features, but goals and terms may differ. An education plan is organised around study costs; compare beneficiaries, term, benefits and withdrawals.
